Your Claude Code sessions in the MacBook notch: see what each one is doing, approve or deny requests, answer Claude's questions, and reply, from any app or screen. Now with a timer, Pomodoro, and a shelf for carrying files and text between apps and Spaces.

### Install
1. Download **ClaudeNotch-0.2.0.dmg** below. It runs natively on Apple-silicon and Intel Macs, macOS 14 or later.
2. Open it and drag **ClaudeNotch** onto **Applications**. If you have 0.1.0, replace it.
3. Open ClaudeNotch. This build isn't notarized by Apple yet, so the first time macOS says it can't verify it. Go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, click **Open Anyway** next to ClaudeNotch, and confirm. You only do this once.

### New in 0.2.0
- **Tabs.** The open notch now has **Claude**, **Timer** and **Shelf**.
- **Timer.** Presets from 1 minute to 1 hour, or nudge with − / +. Pause, resume, reset. The countdown sits beside the notch while it runs, and you get a chime and a 4-second pop-up when it ends.
- **Pomodoro.** 25 min focus, 5 min breaks, a 15 min break after 4 rounds. Breaks start by themselves; the next focus round waits for you.
- **Shelf.** Drag files, images, links or text onto the notch from any app, switch Space or app, then drag them back out or click to copy. **Paste** adds what you copied last. Files stay where they are, and the shelf empties when ClaudeNotch quits.
- **Clipboard history**, off by default. Turn on **History** in the shelf to keep everything you copy. macOS asks first: set ClaudeNotch to **Allow** under **Privacy & Security → Paste from Other Apps**. Items password managers mark as private are never kept.
- Timers survive restarting the app.

Everything from 0.1.0 is still there: colour-coded sessions, Allow / Deny / Always allow, answers to Claude's questions, plan review, replies from the notch, and 120 fps animation at about 0.5% CPU when idle.

Needs Claude Code (desktop app or CLI). Everything stays on your Mac.
